Welp, that's it, I didn't get the Steam Frame :(
<Image src={wtlst} alt={'waitlist'} style={{ '--w' : '75%' }} />

Ever since Valve announced the new Controller, Machine, and Frame in November, I've been saving every single little bit of money I get in preparation to order one. **That turned out to be completely pointless.**
Now, I'm going to have to wait until next year to even think about ordering one.

A similar thing happened with the Controller, but I'm less disappointed about that as I didn't reserve one initially or try order one before the reservations were even a thing. I only placed an order a couple months ago and I expect that to not come for a long while. It's not something I especially *need*, but it'd be nice to have one but I still hope my order for that comes in soon too.

<Image src={cntrl} alt={'controller'} />

I'm just especially pissed about the Frame because I had actually been saving for it for a whole fucking year. I had been following each and every update on [SteamDB](https://steamdb.info/app/4165890/history/) waiting so patiently for it to finally release, every monday waiting for 6pm to see if it releases.

I had gotten so psyched up about it, saving up so much and to finally get some Steam hardware, only for scalpers to get orders first and put listings up on eBay before they even get their own orders. **So fucking stupid.**

And now, I have no fucking idea what year I'll even get the thing. Unfortunately, I'm expecting it to be next year much like with my late order of the Controller. And now, I have to wait even longer; first a year to even reserve, to waiting for an order, to now waiting in the waitlist.

I'm still going to hold onto my saved up money, I'm not letting that go anywhere, most definitely not towards a Meta Quest, I fucking **hate** Meta and I'd hate to have to resort to getting a shitty Quest, or go through eBay to get a discontinued Quest with face-tracking. Bigscreen Beyond exists too but I'm not at all interested in that either, nothing against them as a company but just simply not interested.

If I was to get a Frame order, I would've absolutely ordered the [colour-passthrough camera](https://arcturus.vision/), that thing is extremely interesting. Perhaps I'll order one and keep it somewhere for when/if I do get a Frame.

But for now, more waiting. We'll see what happens from here.
